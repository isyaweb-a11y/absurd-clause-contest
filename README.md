One sentence, at most 200 characters: an absurd clause buried deep inside
a terms-of-service agreement — entered in the
[Absurd Clause Contest](https://make.fun/bounty/u-donrulezz-the-absurd-clause-contest-035cc0f1)
bounty on make.fun.

## The clause

> Section 7.1: If you sneeze while using this service, you agree to bless yourself out loud within three (3) business days.

It reads as real boilerplate right up until it doesn't: a numbered
section, a condition, an obligation — and the obligation is to bless
yourself, out loud, on a business-days deadline. The joke is the register
itself.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) validates the clause — 1 to 200
characters, not only white space — and writes it to `/output/entry.txt`.

```sh
python3 main.py
```
